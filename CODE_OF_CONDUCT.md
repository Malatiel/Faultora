# Code of Conduct

Faultora welcomes participation regardless of experience, background,
identity or ability. This code applies to project issues, pull requests,
reviews and other project-managed discussions, and when representing the
project in public.

## Expected behavior

- Treat people respectfully. Critique ideas and behavior with specific facts.
- Make room for questions, differing experience and constructive disagreement.
- Respect privacy and boundaries. Share another person's information only
  with their permission.
- Keep discussion relevant, and accept reasonable moderation requests.

Harassment, threats, discriminatory remarks, sexual attention or content that
is unwelcome, personal attacks, doxxing and repeated disruption are not
acceptable. Technical disagreement is welcome; abuse of a participant is not.

## Reporting and moderation

For abusive content on GitHub, use GitHub's reporting tools. For a concern
that needs project moderation, open an issue with a brief, non-sensitive
request for moderation, linking public project content when appropriate.
Do not post personal details, private messages or sensitive evidence in a
public issue. There is currently no dedicated private conduct-reporting
inbox; if private follow-up is needed, ask a maintainer to arrange a suitable
channel before sharing it. GitHub's reporting tools remain an option when a
project-level report is not appropriate, including concerns about a
maintainer. Vulnerabilities use the separate [security policy](SECURITY.md).

Maintainers may ask participants to change their behavior, edit or remove
disruptive content, close or lock a discussion, or restrict participation.
They should consider the context, severity and recurrence, explain decisions
where doing so respects privacy, and avoid retaliation against people who
raise concerns in good faith. This code applies to maintainers too. No
response deadline or confidentiality of public issues is promised.
